HOMA-IR Calculator

Calculate your Insulin Resistance using the HOMA-IR formula.

HOMA-IR = fasting insulin (µU/mL) × fasting glucose (mmol/L) ÷ 22.5, which equals insulin × glucose (mg/dL) ÷ 405. There is no single universal cut-off: reported thresholds vary with the population and the insulin assay, so interpret the result against a reference appropriate to your laboratory and patient group.

Source: Matthews DR, Hosker JP, Rudenski AS, Naylor BA, Treacher DF, Turner RC. Homeostasis model assessment: insulin resistance and β-cell function from fasting plasma glucose and insulin concentrations in man. Diabetologia. 1985;28(7):412–419. PMID 3899825.
