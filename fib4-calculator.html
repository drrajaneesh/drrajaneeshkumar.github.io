FIB-4 Calculator

Non-invasive index of the likelihood of advanced liver fibrosis (F3–F4).

FIB-4 = (age × AST) ÷ (platelets × √ALT). Cutoffs: below 1.45 low probability of advanced fibrosis; 1.45 to 3.25 indeterminate; above 3.25 high probability. The index was derived in adults with HIV/HCV co-infection and is applied to other chronic liver disease; it is less specific in people aged 65 or over and may be unreliable in acute hepatitis, markedly raised transaminases, or conditions affecting platelet count.

Source: Sterling RK, Lissen E, Clumeck N, et al. Development of a simple noninvasive index to predict significant fibrosis in patients with HIV/HCV coinfection. Hepatology. 2006;43(6):1317–1325. Guideline endorsement of these cutoffs has not been re-verified for this page; check the current local or society guidance before acting.
